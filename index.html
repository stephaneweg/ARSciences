AR Sciences – Build molecules with your bare hands

A mixed-reality chemistry game for Meta Quest. Pinch atoms out of the periodic table, bring them together, and watch real molecules take shape on your own table.

Meta Quest 3 · 3S Hand tracking Mixed reality + VR English · Français Works offline

How it plays

Your hands are the tools

No controllers. Tap an element, pinch the atom, and bring two atoms together: they bond by themselves. Pull harder to break a bond.

Your table is the lab

The game finds your real table and wall. The work area sits in front of you, atoms bounce on the tabletop, and a chalkboard hangs on your wall.

Real chemistry

Valences, double and triple bonds, polarity, ionic and metallic bonds. Every molecule you build is recognised, named and explained.

1,000+ molecules to discover

From water to caffeine: each discovery comes with its formula and a short description, in English and French, available offline.

Missions, ranks, daily challenge

Seventy missions take you from the first molecules to sugars, acids and metals. Climb the ranks and come back for the challenge of the day.

A collection that grows

Every molecule you discover is kept under a glass dome in the collection tower that rises out of your table. Pinch one to take it out again.

AR Sciences – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

AR Sciences is a mixed-reality chemistry game for Meta Quest, developed by Stephan Wegener ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. In short: the app does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. AR Sciences has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not operate any server that receives data from the app.

Device features used by the app

To work, the app uses the following Meta Quest features. The corresponding data is processed on your headset only, in real time, and is never recorded by the app or sent anywhere.

  • Hand tracking – the position of your hands and fingers, so you can grab atoms and press buttons.
  • Passthrough – the view of your surroundings is displayed by the headset's system. The app has no access to the camera images.
  • Scene data (spatial data) – the position and size of the table and walls you scanned during your headset's space setup, so the lab can be placed on your table and the board on your wall. The app asks for your permission before reading this data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on the headset: completed missions, discovered molecules, the layout of the lab on your table, and your language and display settings. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Internet access

The game is designed to work offline: molecule names and descriptions are included in the app. Some versions of the app may look up the name or description of a molecule you built on public reference services (Wikipedia, Wikidata, PubChem). Such a request contains only the name or chemical structure of the molecule, and no information about you. Like any website, these services receive your IP address when contacted; their own privacy policies apply.

Children

AR Sciences does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Meta platform

The app is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may collect data about your use of the headset and the store under its own terms; please refer to Meta's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
